    Check AI output before you use it

    AI tools can state wrong information with confidence. Check facts, figures, names and quotations yourself before you send or publish anything an AI tool wrote.

    reviews AI-written work before it leaves the organization.

    A person decides about people

    Don’t use an AI tool to decide who is hired or who is eligible for a service. A person makes that decision, and the person affected can ask for a review.
