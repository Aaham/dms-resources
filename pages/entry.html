AI readiness scorecard

Rate 20 statements about how your organization uses AI. You get a score out of 40, a score for each of five areas and suggested next steps, in a report you can download. We also email you the report.

Cybersecurity and AI governance: a guide for leaders

Eighteen steps for leadership, nine on cybersecurity and nine on AI governance, drawing on guidance from NIST and CISA. Each part ends with questions to ask your IT provider or your AI vendors. Read it on this site or download the 8-page PDF.

One-page incident plan

Fill in who decides and who to call if your organization is hacked, locked out of its systems or tricked into sending money. You get one page to print, with nine steps and your own names and phone numbers on it.

AI rules for staff

List the AI tools your staff may use and the information that must stay out of them. You get a one-page handout with six rules, to print or paste into your own document.

Board and executive AI brief

Seven questions for leadership to answer before approving a new use of AI, from the problem it solves to who can stop it. You get a one-page brief to print for your board or executive team.

AI vendor questions

Twenty-five questions to send a vendor before you buy or renew an AI tool, on how your information is used, kept, secured and deleted. You record each answer and your decision, then print the record.
