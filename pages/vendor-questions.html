AI vendor questions

Send these questions to a vendor before you buy, renew or expand an AI tool, or before sensitive information goes into one. Record each answer and where it is written, then your decision.

What you type is saved in this browser. It is not sent to DMS.

The vendor and the tool

Public is already published. Internal is not for release. Sensitive is about clients, staff, donors, finances, health, immigration or location. Regulated is covered by a law, a grant, a contract or a professional duty.

Send the vendor all 25 questions. Ask for each answer in the contract or the vendor’s written terms, not only from a salesperson.

As the answers come back, type each one under its question with where it is written, such as the clause number. Then choose your view of it.

The tool
  1. Which parts of the product use AI, and what do your terms say we may not use it for?
  2. How have you tested the product for accuracy and for bias, and can we see the results?
How our information is used
  1. Do you use what we enter, what the product produces for us, our feedback or our usage data to train or improve any AI model?
  2. Do you use our information for any purpose other than providing the service to us?
  3. Is our information kept separate from other customers’ information?
  4. Which of your staff can read what we enter, and in what circumstances?
Other companies and locations
  1. Which other companies receive our information, including the AI model provider and the hosting company?
  2. In which countries is our information stored and processed?
  3. If a law, a grant or a contract covers our information, will you sign the agreement it requires, such as a HIPAA business associate agreement?
Keeping, deleting and exporting
  1. How long do you keep what we enter, what the product produces, logs and backups?
  2. Can we set a shorter period, or delete items ourselves?
  3. When the contract ends, how and when is our information deleted, including copies and backups?
  4. Can we export our information and our records of decisions in a format we can use elsewhere?
Security
  1. Does the product support multi-factor authentication, and can an administrator control what each person can see and do?
  2. Is our information encrypted when it is sent and when it is stored?
  3. Can you send us a current independent security report or certificate, such as SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001?
  4. Can we get logs showing who used the product, what they did, what was changed and which sources an answer drew on?
When something goes wrong
  1. How soon must you tell us about a security incident or an unauthorized disclosure of our information?
  2. What information and help will you give us to investigate, and to notify the people affected?
  3. Who is responsible under the contract when a result is inaccurate, discriminatory, harmful or infringes someone’s rights?
Ownership, changes and ending the contract
  1. Do we own what we enter, what the product produces for us, our settings and any knowledge base we build in it?
  2. Which of your confidentiality duties continue after the contract ends?
  3. How much notice will we get before you change the AI model, the features, the price, the terms, or the companies and countries that handle our information?
  4. If we don’t accept a change, can we end the contract without a penalty and take our information with us?
  5. What does the contract say about your insurance, your liability to us, and our rights to audit you and to end the contract early?
Decision

The person responsible for how the tool is used, not only a technical contact.

Red flags

  • “We are compliant”, with no evidence.
  • No clear answer on whether your information trains a model.
  • Information kept indefinitely, or no commitment to delete it.
  • The other companies that receive your information are not named.
  • You would hear about an incident only after it is public.
  • No way to export your information.
  • The vendor can change important terms without your agreement.
  • The contract puts all the responsibility on you.
